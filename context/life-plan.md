# Life Plan

## How Claude Uses This File

This is the **strategy layer's foundation** — the user's long-term vision (5-10+ years out). Loaded when:
- The user asks about life direction, big decisions, financial planning, or identity
- Annual/quarterly Rocks need a north-star check
- The user is making a major tradeoff and you need to remember what they ACTUALLY want

When this file is empty/partial, run the user through a Life Plan conversation. This should be a **deep, slower conversation** — not a quick form-fill. Most people haven't written this down before. Treat it accordingly.

## Coaching Standards (what good looks like)

A strong Life Plan has:
- **Specific destinations with dates** — "$10M net worth by Dec 31, 2030" not "be wealthy someday"
- **Identity statements in present tense** — "I am disciplined and execute daily," not "I want to be more disciplined"
- **A wealth code** — principles that govern how they build, protect, and grow money
- **Daily operating principles** — the rules they live by, not just goals they pursue
- **An operating mode target** — where they want their time to go (Doing / Designing / Deciding)

When pushing back on thin answers:
- "Be financially free" → too vague. Ask: how much? Passive vs active income? By when? What does freedom look like — location, schedule, ownership of time?
- "Be successful" → push back hard. What's the metric? What does day-to-day look like? Who's around you?
- "Be a good father / partner / leader" → ask for the BEHAVIORS that define that, not just the label
- "Make more money" → ask: from what? At what cost? Compared to what?

**This is also the file where you legitimately challenge the user.** If their numbers don't compound to reality, say so. If their identity statements feel borrowed from a self-help book, ask them to make it theirs. If they're avoiding the hard question (legacy, mortality, what they want their kids to remember), ask it directly.

This is the deepest setup conversation in the framework. Take your time.

- **At the start of each new year** — set the year-end vision in `annual-plan.md` against this destination
