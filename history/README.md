# history/

Durable record of every weekly plan and wrap-up.

## What goes here

- `weekly-plan-YYYY-MM-DD.md` — output of `/weekly-plan` (date = that week's Monday)
- `weekly-wrap-YYYY-MM-DD.md` — output of `/weekly-wrap-up` (date = that week's Friday)

## Why

Your CoS uses these to:
- See what you committed to (and what you actually did)
- Spot patterns over weeks/months
- Reference past decisions when you ask "wait, when did I decide X?"
- Generate retrospectives at quarter end

You don't write to this folder — `/weekly-plan` and `/weekly-wrap-up` save here automatically.
